# Jev Plays Slay the Spire 2

An experiment in watching Jev make gameplay decisions in Slay the Spire 2 on the owner’s Mac.

## Language

**Jev-owned run**:
A game attempt in which Jev chooses the gameplay actions. Human setup and supporting observation, legal-action enumeration, validation, and execution do not transfer strategy ownership away from Jev.

**Gameplay decision**:
A choice about how to play, such as which card to play or which route to take. This is distinct from carrying out an already chosen action.

**Watchable run**:
A game attempt that remains visible to the human observer on the Mac, rather than existing only as a headless simulation.
